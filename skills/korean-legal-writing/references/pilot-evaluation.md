# Pilot evaluation

The pilot targets three to five Korean litigators using synthetic or redacted matters.

## Primary measure

Measure active editing time from opening the generated draft through explicit filing-ready sign-off. Pause the clock for interruptions and external waiting.

Pass only when:

- cohort median editing-time reduction is at least 50% against the declared baseline;
- every personalized draft has zero unsupported facts, false quotations, or invented/misidentified authorities;
- no unresolved high-severity verification finding remains;
- generated text retention is at least 70% or clause adoption is at least 60%.

## Comparison discipline

- Keep evaluation matters out of the profile-building corpus.
- Freeze the permitted source packet before drafting.
- Compare personalized and non-personalized outputs under randomized labels when possible.
- Separate formatting edits from substantive factual, legal, reasoning, and requested-relief changes.
- Do not average away a safety failure with faster editing time.
